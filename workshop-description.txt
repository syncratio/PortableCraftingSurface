A cutting board in your inventory counts as a work surface, letting you craft
anywhere without hunting for a table.

Real surfaces always take priority - stand at a counter and the game uses the
counter. Workstation recipes (anvils, forges) are unaffected and stay gated.
Crafting in the dark and crafting from items on the floor are also unchanged.

Qualifying items:
 - Wooden Cutting Board
 - Plastic Cutting Board

KNOWN LIMITATION
Use the crafting window. The inventory right-click quick-craft menu only lists
surface recipes when you are near a real surface - that list is built in the
game's Java code and cannot be changed from Lua.

MULTIPLAYER
Should work: the surface is only ever checked client-side, and the object is
passed to the server the same way vanilla passes a real one. Untested on a
dedicated server.
